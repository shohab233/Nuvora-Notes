# Nuvora Notes 📝

**Nuvora Notes** is a modern, lightweight, privacy-focused, and elegant note-taking & checklist app for Android.

---

### ✨ Features
* 🎨 **Material 3 Design:** Beautiful Indigo palette and 12 eye-friendly pastel card colors.
* 📝 **Rich Text Formatting:** Bold, Italics, Monospace, Strikethrough, and clickable links.
* 📋 **To-Do & Checklists:** Organize your daily tasks effortlessly.
* 🖼️ **Media Attachments:** Add images and voice audio recordings directly into notes.
* 📑 **Flexible Export:** Export notes as PDF, TXT, HTML, or JSON.
* 🔒 **Local & Private:** 100% offline local storage with automatic backups. No data tracking.
* 📌 **Pinning & Labels:** Organize with custom color labels and pinned notes.
* 📱 **Home Screen Widgets & Reminders:** Stay on top of your priorities.
